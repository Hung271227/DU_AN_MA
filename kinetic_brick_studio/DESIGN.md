## Brand & Style

This design system powers a premier authentic LEGO retail platform engineered for high-energy commerce, collector prestige, and creative spark. The aesthetic balances retail precision with structural playfulness, avoiding childish motifs in favor of sophisticated industrial design, crisp tactile surfaces, and gallery-grade presentation.

### Aesthetic Attributes
- **Dynamic Tactile Pop:** High visual contrast using signature primary brick tones against ultra-clean, clinical white backdrops.
- **Architectural Clarity:** Modular card geometry mimicking precise structural interlocks, with generous breathing space.
- **Collector Prestige:** Verified authenticity seals, metallic collector accents, and museum-grade showcase layouts.

## Colors

The palette leverages iconic brick pigments anchored by high-grade industrial neutrals.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#E52521`):** Action-driving brand red. Reserved for key transactional triggers, core focal badges, and decisive brand identity.
- **Secondary (`#FFD500`):** Kinetic brick yellow. Deployed strategically for promotional badges, loyalty tags, star ratings, and celebratory accents.
- **Tertiary (`#0055BF`):** Classic brick cobalt. Utilized for technical specifications, structural filter states, and informational callouts.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:** Absolute White (`#FFFFFF`) forms the primary operational ground, supported by Warm Gallery Alabaster (`#F8F9FA`) for nested module layers.
- **Neutral Text & Structure (`#1E1E1E` & `#2D3748`):** Deep charcoal and carbon slate for high-clarity legibility, eliminating pure harsh black in favor of dense mineral tones.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between geometric energy and technical precision:

- **Headlines & Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its friendly yet structural geometric terminals, wide apertures, and punchy extra-bold weights that resonate with industrial product design.
- **Labels, Specs & Badges (Space Grotesk):** Provides an authentic engineering dimension suited for piece counts, item serial numbers, age-group tags, and retail badges. Uppercase treatments with tracking ensure instant readability in micro-spaces.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column fluid grid system with fixed responsive margins anchors the catalog and merchandising flows.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-mobile` (1rem / 16px). Product listings default to a compact 2-column grid.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px), `margin` (2rem / 32px).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, max-width container capped at 1360px to maintain optical control over dense product catalogs.

Spacing between modular retail components follows an 8pt base grid for proportional stacking.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages soft architectural diffusion rather than harsh drop shadows, maintaining a clean tactile atmosphere.

- **Level 0 (Floor):** Neutral background `#F8F9FA`. Used for page backing and full-bleed utility strips.
- **Level 1 (Card Resting):** Solid `#FFFFFF` elevated with a double-pass shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.02)`. Borders use low-contrast ghost lines (`1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces):** Lifted state with `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(229, 37, 33, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.05)`, creating an ambient, red-tinted kinetic glow.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Menus & Cart Modals):** Pure white with backdrop-blur support (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `16px blur`) and `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a unified structural curvature:
- Standard interactive elements (inputs, select menus, action buttons) utilize **0.5rem** (`rounded-md`).
- Primary content containers, gallery showcases, and modular product cards adopt **1rem** (`rounded-lg`).
- High-visibility promotional banners, floating drawer trays, and hero modules expand to **1.5rem** (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro-badges, price stickers, and status pills adopt full pill geometry (`rounded-full`) for high-contrast shape distinction against rectangular cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Checkout):** Background `#E52521`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 700. Inset top highlight border (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`) to echo physical brick injection molding. Hover states trigger a subtle -2px vertical translation with enhanced crimson diffusion.
- **Secondary Action (Quick View / Explore):** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E1E1E`, outline `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover shifts background to `#F8F9FA` with border color snapping to `#1E1E1E`.
- **Tertiary Accent (Loyalty / Rewards):** Background `#FFD500`, text `#1E1E1E`, tracking bold, high-contrast black icons.

### Product Badges & Stickers
- **Official Authentic:** Deep graphite ground (`#1E1E1E`) paired with metallic gold typography, certified checkmark icon, pill-shaped.
- **Sale / Hot:** Pure `#E52521` with crisp white uppercase `Space Grotesk` text, positioned on the top-left card anchor.
- **New Release:** `#FFD500` background with `#1E1E1E` bold text.
- **Piece Count & Age Badges:** Transparent white pill with frosted glass blur (`backdrop-blur-md`), positioned bottom-right within product image frames.

### Product Cards
- Built on Level 1 elevation with smooth 200ms transition curves.
- Features square 1:1 image containers with ultra-subtle inset background `#F8F9FA`.
- Persistent bottom row displaying piece count, item code in `Space Grotesk`, prominent price in `#1E1E1E`, and quick-action icon button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Focus state delivers a 0px offset `3px` focus ring in `#E52521` at 20% opacity, with border turning `#E52521`.

### Filter Chips
- Capsule-style chips with `#F1F5F9` background and `#2D3748` typography. Selected state transitions to solid `#1E1E1E` with `#FFFFFF` text.
